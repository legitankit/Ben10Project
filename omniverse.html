Omniverse - 2012

Omniverse introduces a new art style and a dual timeline, showing both young Ben and 16-year-old Ben. With Gwen in college and Kevin gone, Ben teams up with Rook Blonko, a disciplined alien partner. The Omnitrix unlocks new DNA while dangerous enemies target its power. They face villains like Khyber, Malware, and time-manipulating threats. The story blends humor with high-stakes action and time-travel complications. Omniverse highlights Ben’s growth into a confident, experienced hero while exploring the deeper legacy and impact of the Omnitrix.
