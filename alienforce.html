Alien Force - 2008

Years after his childhood adventures, Ben Tennyson returns to hero life when Grandpa Max mysteriously disappears while investigating an alien plot. Now 15, Ben reunites with Gwen and Kevin Levin to find him. The Omnitrix recalibrates, giving Ben a new set of powerful alien forms. Unlike the lighter tone of the original series, this story begins darker and more serious. The team soon uncovers a hidden invasion by the Highbreed, a species that sees humanity as inferior and plans Earth’s destruction. Along the way, they battle DNAliens, alien warlords, and intergalactic enemies. The fights become more strategic and intense, focusing on teamwork, responsibility, and Ben learning to act not as a kid with powers—but as a true leader.
