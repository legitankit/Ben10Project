CLASSIC CONTINUITY

BEN 10 CLASSIC

Classic Continuity refers to the original storyline of the Ben 10 series that started in 2005. In this continuity, Ben Tennyson, a 10-year-old boy, discovers the Omnitrix, a device that allows him to transform into various aliens. The story follows Ben as he travels with his cousin Gwen and Grandpa Max, battling villains like Vilgax and learning responsibility and heroism along the way. Classic Continuity includes the original four series: Ben 10 (2005), Alien Force, Ultimate Alien, and Omniverse. These shows maintain a consistent timeline, character development, and overarching narrative, expanding Ben’s universe gradually while keeping the original tone of adventure and humor.
